About AAA Tailor Shop

Expert tailoring, alterations, and clothing repairs in Albuquerque.

Our Craft

Our tailors specialize in tailoring, alterations, clothing repairs, and custom work for formal and casual wear for men, women, and children.

All work is completed on-site by professional tailors with exceptional attention to quality and detail. Our goal is simple: ensure your garments fit perfectly and make you look and feel your best.

From simple repairs to full tailoring adjustments, we guarantee that every piece is handled with the care it deserves.

Our Mission

To provide old-fashioned customer service, the finest custom clothing, and create happy, well-dressed customers for life.

Our Goal

Deliver superior customer service so every client leaves completely satisfied with their final garment at an affordable price.

Why Choose Us?

We love clothes and we love helping people look their best. Every garment is treated with enthusiasm and care so it can make a lasting impression once again.

Our Commitment To You

AAA Tailor Shop values every customer. Our loyal clients throughout Albuquerque trust our professionalism, ethics, and dedication to quality workmanship.

From replacing a button to hemming a formal gown, every garment is treated as if it were our own. Our reputation depends on every alteration we perform — and we take that responsibility seriously.
